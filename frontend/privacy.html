Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2025

Who we are

Rider Assistant is an unofficial chatbot created by an independent user for educational purposes, designed to help Uber Eats riders in the Netherlands consult contractual and regulatory documentation. It has no affiliation with Uber, Adecco, or any other legal entity, and is not a substitute for them. All documentation used to inform the chatbot is publicly available and has been supplemented with notes based on personal experience: as such, those may be outdated, inaccurate, or incorrect. Always double check. If you notice missing or incorrect info, or you want to suggest improvements, contact: unofficial.uechatbot.assistanc@gmail.com

Data collected

When you use the chat, the following information is recorded:

  • The text of your question
  • The detected language
  • The response generated by the system
  • The internal documents consulted to produce the response

No name, email address, IP address, or other identifying data is collected.

Purpose of processing

Data is used exclusively to improve the quality of the service: identifying frequent questions without adequate answers and detecting gaps in the available documentation.

Retention

Conversation logs are retained for a maximum of 90 days, after which they are automatically deleted.

Legal basis

Processing is based on the legitimate interest of the controller (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) in improving the service, balanced against users' rights. No sensitive data within the meaning of Art. 9 GDPR is processed.

Data sharing

Data is not shared with third parties, nor sold or transferred. The database is hosted on cloud infrastructure with servers located in Europe.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you may request access to, rectification of, or deletion of your data by contacting: unofficial.uechatbot.assistanc@gmail.com
